# Breach runbook
Roles: Incident lead (tech lead), Communications (PM), Client contact, Legal/DPO (client side).
T+0h Identify and contain; T+2h scope and preliminary impact; T+24h decision on notification with reasoning recorded; T+72h notification deadline where personal data at risk; T+7d post-incident report with timeline and preventive actions.
Templates: internal notice (what, when, systems, actions), client notice (facts, impact, actions, next update time), authority notification fields (nature, categories and approximate numbers, consequences, measures, DPO contact).
Never: delete logs, delay rotation for convenience, communicate speculation as fact.
